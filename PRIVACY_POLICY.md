# Gear Forge — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-31_

This privacy policy applies to the Gear Forge Android application ("the app"),
package `com.gearforge.geargenerator`.

## 1. What we do NOT collect

Gear Forge does **not** require an account and does **not** collect, store or
transmit any of the following:

- Names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details.
- Location data.
- Photos, contacts or files from your device (exported gear files are written to
  your own Downloads folder and are never uploaded).
- Health, financial or other sensitive personal data.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays Google AdMob **rewarded video ads** at the export gate. AdMob and
its partners may use the device's **advertising identifier (Ad ID)** and other
device information to serve and measure ads, including **personalized ads**.

- For users in the EEA/UK and other applicable regions, we show Google's UMP
  (User Messaging Platform) consent form before any ads are loaded, and the app
  respects your consent choice.
- You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings
  (Settings → Google → Ads), or via Google's Ads Settings.

Google's use of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## 3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers a one-time "Pro" in-app purchase via **Google Play Billing**. Payment
processing is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees or stores your
payment card details. Your purchase is tied to your Google Account and can be
restored on other devices signed in to the same account.

## 4. Data stored on device

The app stores only lightweight local preferences on your device (theme, language,
units, Pro status, remaining free-export count) using Android SharedPreferences.
This data never leaves your device.

## 5. Children

The app is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data
from children.

## 6. Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be reflected
in-app.

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: <gearforge.app@gmail.com>.

## Svensk sammanfattning (Swedish summary)

Gear Forge samlar **inte in** personuppgifter, kräver inget konto och laddar inte upp
dina filer. Appen visar **belöningsannonser via Google AdMob** vid export, vilket kan
använda enhetens **reklam-ID** för att visa och mäta annonser (inklusive
personanpassade annonser). Inom EES/EU visas Googles samtyckesformulär (UMP) innan
annonser laddas. Pro-köpet hanteras helt av **Google Play Billing** — appen ser aldrig
dina kortuppgifter. Endast lokala inställningar (tema, språk, enheter, Pro-status och
antal kvarvarande gratisexporter) sparas på din enhet.

## För publicering (publishing checklist)

1. Host this file at a publicly accessible URL.
2. Enter that URL in Google Play Console → App content → Privacy policy.
3. Complete the Play Console **Data Safety** form so it matches the disclosures above.
